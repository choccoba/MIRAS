## 3. Specific Utilization Guidelines

### Preferred Tasks
1. **Deep Progress & Consistency Audit**  
   Long-running projects (MIRAS, TCGA-LIHC, ITLAS, etc.): simultaneously read logs, handoffs, MasterPlans, results files and produce a coherent “where we are + contradictions” summary.

2. **Multi-source Biomedical Verification**  
   GEO accession status / license / public availability, HMDD/dbDEMC cross-check, multi-paper consistency, data embargo assessment.

3. **Secondary Adversarial Review** (pre-GPT filter)  
   Critically examine Opus drafts or analysis results before they go to GPT-5.6 SOL. Focus on:  
   - “Is this claim sufficiently supported by multi-dataset evidence?”  
   - “Is there over-interpretation of concordance?”  
   - “Are limitations and embargoes transparently stated?”

4. **Manuscript Section Support**  
   Role-divided drafting of Results / Discussion subsections → leader integrates. Internal multi-view discussion produces richer first drafts.

5. **Personal Helper & 2nd Opinion**  
   Rapid synthesis, decision trade-off analysis, next-Work-Order prioritization, interpretation of Cursor outputs.

### Tasks to Avoid or Limit
- Primary high-level paper design or final manuscript writing (Opus domain)
- Heavy continuous code execution / long R sessions (Cursor ExtraFast domain)
- Top-tier adversarial leadership (GPT-5.6 SOL domain)
